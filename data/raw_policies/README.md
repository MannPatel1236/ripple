Drop raw cancellation-policy text files here (one .txt per policy), then run:

    python3 ../../scripts/parse_policies.py .

Collect for SHAPE VARIETY, not volume:
  - slab-based      (IRCTC, airline fee tables) — hardest, most impressive
  - binary          ("Non-refundable")
  - single cutoff   ("Free until 48h before")
  - percentage      ("50% refund within 7 days")
  - fee + partial   (change fee plus fare difference)
  - messy prose     (terms buried mid-paragraph, no table) — the proof asset
